Create and manage professional invoices directly from your WordPress admin panel.

== Description ==

Invoice Creator allows you to create, manage, and send professional invoices directly from your WordPress dashboard. Generate beautiful, customizable PDF invoices for your clients with ease.

Features:
* Create professional invoices with your business branding
* Add your company logo
* Support for multiple currencies
* Add itemized line items with quantity, rate, and discounts
* Calculate taxes and shipping
* Support for multiple payment methods
* Download invoices as PDFs
* Track payment status

== Installation ==

1. Upload the 'invoice-creator' folder to the '/wp-content/plugins/' directory
2. Activate the plugin through the 'Plugins' menu in WordPress
3. Navigate to 'Invoice Creator' in your admin menu to start creating invoices

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Can I customize the invoice template? =
Yes, you can add your logo and customize business details.

= What payment methods are supported? =
The plugin supports Bank Transfer, PayPal, UPI, Payment Link, and Cash payment methods.

= Can I download the invoices as PDFs? =
Yes, you can generate and download your invoices as professional PDF documents.
